## Set up, secure, and operate your device fleet

Explore the core capabilities of Microsoft Intune and learn how to manage, secure, and support devices across your organization.

![](media/icons/hub-page/get-started.svg)[Get started](fundamentals/)
Set up your Intune environment, understand architecture and licensing, and deploy your first policies.

![](media/icons/hub-page/device-enrollment.svg)[Enroll devices](device-enrollment/)
Onboard Windows, Apple, Android, and Linux devices using automated, user-driven, or bulk enrollment methods.

![](media/icons/hub-page/device-configuration.svg)[Configure devices](device-configuration/)
Push settings, restrictions, and platform templates to devices with configuration profiles and the settings catalog.

![](media/icons/hub-page/app-management.svg)[Manage apps](app-management/)
Add, deploy, configure, and protect apps across managed and unmanaged devices using app policies and assignments.

![](media/icons/hub-page/rbac.svg)[Govern and administer](govern-administer)
Configure role-based access, scope tags, audit logs, and tenant-wide policies to operate Intune at scale.

![](media/icons/hub-page/device-updates.svg)[Keep devices updated](device-updates/)
Manage OS and driver updates for Windows, iOS, macOS, and Android devices with update rings, expedited updates, and policies.

![](media/icons/hub-page/device-security.svg)[Secure devices and data](device-security/)
Enforce compliance, deploy security baselines, integrate Defender for Endpoint, and manage endpoint security policies.

![](media/icons/hub-page/app-protection.svg)[Protect apps and data](app-management/protection/)
Safeguard corporate data with app protection policies, conditional launch rules, and mobile application management for managed and unmanaged devices.

![](media/icons/hub-page/device-management.svg)[Manage devices](device-management/)
Perform remote actions, manage device inventory and lifecycle, and provide remote assistance to users.

![](media/icons/hub-page/reports.svg)[Monitor and troubleshoot](monitor-troubleshoot)
Track device health, compliance trends, and app deployment status with built-in reports and Endpoint Analytics.

![](media/icons/hub-page/copilot.svg)[Copilot and agents](/en-us/intune/copilot/index)
Use AI-powered Copilot and autonomous agents to query device data, troubleshoot issues, and streamline IT workflows.

![](media/icons/hub-page/developer.svg)[Develop and automate](developer/)
Use Microsoft Graph APIs, PowerShell, and the Intune Data Warehouse to automate management tasks and build custom integrations.
